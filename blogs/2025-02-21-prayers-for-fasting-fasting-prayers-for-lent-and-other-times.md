Fasting and prayer have a strong connection, particularly during the season of Lent. Along with almsgiving (charity), fasting and prayer make up the pillars of Lent. Ultimately, Christians seek to fast from sin; prayer and building our relationship with God, can also help us fast from a life of sin.
